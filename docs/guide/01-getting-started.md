# 1. Getting Started

This guide covers the basics everyone needs: opening the app, logging in, changing
your password, finding your way around, and logging out.

[← Back to the manual index](README.md)

---

## 1.1 Opening the app

The app runs on your organization's internal network. Your administrator will give
you a **web address** (it looks something like `http://server-name` or
`http://192.168.x.x`).

1. Open a web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox).
2. Type the address your administrator gave you into the address bar and press **Enter**.
3. The **Login** screen appears.

> 📷 **Screenshot placeholder:** _The login screen showing Username and Password fields._

> 💡 **Tip:** Save the address as a browser bookmark so you don't have to type it
> each time.

---

## 1.2 Getting your account

You do **not** create your own account. An **administrator** creates it for you and
gives you:

- a **username**, and
- a **temporary password**.

The first time you log in, the app will usually ask you to **change this temporary
password** to one only you know.

---

## 1.3 Logging in

1. On the Login screen, type your **username**.
2. Type your **password**.
3. Click **Log In**.

> 📷 **Screenshot placeholder:** _Login form with username/password filled in and the Log In button._

### If the app asks you to change your password

On a first login (or after an admin resets your password), you'll be taken to a
**Change Password** screen. This is normal.

1. Enter your **current (temporary) password**.
2. Enter your **new password** twice.
3. Click **Save** / **Change Password**.

**Your new password must have:**

- at least **8 characters**,
- at least one **UPPERCASE** letter,
- at least one **lowercase** letter,
- at least one **number**.

_Example of a valid password:_ `Welcome@2025`

## 1.4 If you can't log in

| Problem | What's happening | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Invalid username or password" | The username or password is wrong. | Re-type carefully. Check Caps Lock. |
| Login feels **slower each time** you fail | The app **slows down on purpose** after each wrong attempt, to block guessing. | Wait a moment and try again with the correct details. |
| "Account locked" message | After **5 wrong attempts** the account locks for **15 minutes**. | Wait 15 minutes, or ask an administrator to reset it. |
| You were logged out suddenly | You can only be logged in **on one device at a time**, and the app logs you out after **30 minutes of inactivity**. | Simply log in again. |

> ⚠️ **Note:** Logging in on a second device or browser automatically logs you out
> everywhere else. This is a security feature, not an error.

## 1.5 Finding your way around

After logging in you land on your **home/dashboard**. The main areas you can reach
depend on your role:

- **Menu / Website links** — your personal list of approved websites and tools,
  organized as **Category → Sub-Category → Link**. (See your role guide.)
- **Claims** — your claims workspace (the [End-User Guide](02-end-user-guide.md)
  explains this in full).
- **Admin** — only visible to administrators (the [Admin Guide](04-admin-guide.md)).

> 📷 **Screenshot placeholder:** _The home screen / main navigation after login._

---

## 1.6 Changing your password later

You can change your password any time (you don't have to wait to be asked):

1. Open your **account / profile** menu (usually your name in a corner).
2. Choose **Change Password**.
3. Enter your current password, then your new password twice.
4. Click **Save**.

The same password rules from section 1.3 apply.

---

## 1.7 Logging out

Always log out when you're finished, especially on a shared computer.

1. Open your **account / profile** menu.
2. Click **Log Out**.

> 📷 **Screenshot placeholder:** _The account menu open, showing the Log Out option._

> 💡 The app also logs you out automatically after **30 minutes** with no activity.
